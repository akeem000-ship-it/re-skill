Boot: logos → intro (Enter skips) → title (Enter) → the game's menus (Exhibition → singles, games, sets → characters → difficulty → court) → VS → match. The controls above skip straight to a match.
Faithful parts (verified against the ROM/emulator): ball physics (drag, spin, bounce, fences, net), court dimensions, serve box and in/out rules, scoring with deuce, faults and sets, character stats and the movement formulas derived from them, shot aiming and trajectory tables, swing contact and lunges, CPU AI, the camera, the sound driver, court maps, sprites and colours.
