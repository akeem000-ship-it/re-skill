# Polish
- **Overworld trees** (OPTIONS > STORY, on by default): on the story's Field the trees are no longer painted flat on the ground: every tree stands up in 3D in the courtyard style of the hole view (round leafy crowns in the woods, poplars, palms by the Palm Club, cacti by the Dune Club) where the game paints it, with lawn under it, swaying in a light breeze and casting soft shadows on the paths. Walk behind them and they hide you (your silhouette shows through), and the trees in front of you turn see-through. With them the Field's signposts stand upright, its hills and boulders rise as solid mounds, the club houses are little buildings, the lawn is a fine carpet turf (mown in stripes round the club houses) and the paths have fine grit and a soft raised edge.
- **The Field's woods**: each tree in the woods now reads on its own, as the 2D trees did: a dark purple outline round every crown, shade toward its foot where it meets its neighbours, richer GBC greens with fewer white glints, and every tree its own size, height and shade (some darker, some yellower, some blue-green). The grass tufts among the trees are painted over as lawn and the club houses' sides no longer show lawn.
- **The Field's buildings**: the four club houses are real little houses now, not blocks with the map's picture on them: white walls with the columns, windows and door of the 2D art on the front, a hipped roof of the art's striped tiles with eaves over the walls, a little spire on top and a step at the door; the log cabin in the woods (flat before) stands up with log walls, its window in a gable facing you and a shingle roof running back. No lawn pixels on them, lawn under them, a soft shadow, and they hide you or turn see-through when you walk behind them, as the trees do.
- **No tree in your face**: a tree the camera passes through or stands against is left out, and the trees between the camera and the ball or your golfer (and those so close they would fill the picture) turn see-through for as long as they are in the way, so the low chase of a flight past the woods, a landing behind a tree or a shot from the trees always shows the ball. Their shadows stay.
- **The Field's people and the trees**: on the story's Field a person behind the trees (or a building) is hidden by them now instead of standing over them, and shows through them as a dark blue silhouette, so you never lose anyone; low walls and counters still never hide a person.
- **Deck shot map close up**: zoomed in on a green, the map's grass, fringe and bunker edges are drawn sharp instead of blurred.
- **Dusk and night**: sunsets with a gold, rose and violet band over the horizon and a warm glow toward the sun; the low sun lights the course warmly with cool blue shade, so the greens stay green and the trees no longer go brown; at night moonlight and the floodlights keep the course readable. Your golfer, the pin and the marks stay bright at any hour. Peach's Castle (Hour: Auto) now plays at sunset, deepening to dusk over the round.

# Putting
- **Putting: Green camera** (the default; or Follow): while you aim a putt the camera is low behind the ball looking at the cup, framed to the ball, the cup with its flag and the green's edge, closer on a short putt and higher on a long one so the cup stays readable. The golfer and the caddie step out of the picture (only his shadow stays), a ring and a tick mark the ball and the aim. **The break**: the roll and the stop of a putt struck at 100, 77, 50 and 27 % of the meter (the counts a press can really strike at), each with its roll in feet as the HUD counts feet, labels kept apart; a light grid of the green's slope (a chevron per tile, yellow / orange / red by steepness) only near the line, and a ring round the cup so it stays easy to find on a long putt. They are the game's own putts run on a copy of the game: a putt struck at that point stops exactly at its line's end. When you swing the camera eases back to the Follow camera so you see the stroke and the roll as before; the big meter works as before.
- **Green: Clean** (the default with Grass: Carpet; or Arrows, the look before): the green without the painted arrows: very short dense turf, a subtle mowing pattern, a darker collar round it, the cup with a white liner. Its grass moves only with the **slope**: waves running downhill along each spot's slope, bigger and quicker the steeper, still where it is flat, and a slight still lean, exactly where the game's slope pushes the ball; the wind no longer moves the green's grass (off the green the grass still shows the wind).
- Every number of feet on the page (the pin, the full putt, the break) now uses the HUD's own conversion (feet and tenths under 10 ft, as the HUD).

# Aim and swing helpers
- **A cleaner look for the aim marks**: the flight is a smooth tube, thick near you and thin far off, with a soft dark edge so it reads on sky, sand, grass and water, and a light that runs along it; its shadow is a soft line on the ground with a faint curtain up to the flight and a mark at its highest point. Seen from behind the ball, where the flight is edge-on, a small side view in the corner shows its rise and fall. Where it lands is a target reticle lying on the ground (in 3D in perspective, never too small to see), the roll is a thin dotted line to a small rest ring. The aim line and the 50 y rings are lighter and fade with the distance, the rings numbered only every 100 y and away from the flight. Every number has one compact tag style ("284 y" with a small "carry 280", "376 y  pin", "270 y  club"), and the tags keep clear of each other, the HUD, the golfer and the flight. The putting view's break lines are smooth curves with small rings where they stop and tags like "25 ft  27%". The numbers and places are the same as before.
- **Deck: Shot map** (the default when the phone is held upright; or Art): during golf the deck below the game shows a live map of the hole from above, drawn from the game's own hole data (fairway with mowing stripes, rough, sand, water, the green, out of bounds, trees) and turned so your shot goes up. It shows the ball, the pin, the aim line, rings every 50 y, the wind, and the same shot preview as the picture: the flight from above (wider where it flies higher), the landing reticle, the roll to rest and its tag. On the green it shows the green with the slope of every tile and the four putts' break lines with their stops. It zooms to the ball and the landing area (and the pin when it is near) and pans, zooms and turns smoothly as you aim; in flight the ball's live place and track. The controls stay easy to see and hit: on a tall phone or the Fold's cover screen they sit low with the map darker under them; on the Fold's inner screen the map is between the d-pad and A / B. Menus and the story keep the art.
- **Meter: Big** (the default; or Game): while you swing, the swing meter grows into the middle of the picture as clean HD art and shrinks back after the hit: a glowing MAX mark, the PERFECT line at 0, a fading trail behind the cursor, the power you set ("90% · 242 y") and NICE! on a perfect impact. Its speed and timing are the game's own; the golfer stays in view. In 2D, HD and 3D, on desktops and phones.
- **Preview: On** (the default; or Off): while you aim, a helper shows where a **perfect full-power swing** would go with this club, aim, lie and wind: a dotted arc, the landing ring, the roll to rest and "carry X y · Y y" (a putt: the roll line). It is the game's own shot code run on a copy of the game, so a perfect full swing lands exactly there; a softer or mistimed swing goes elsewhere.
- **Markers: On** (the default; or Off): the aim line to the target marker, a ring there with the club's yards, rings every 50 y, the distance to the pin over the flag; marks off the picture point from its edge.
- **Tap area** (touch screens, On by default): at the address and during the swing the whole lower half of the screen is the A button; the d-pad (hook / slice) and B still work.

# The 3D view
- **3D view** (the option above): the hole the game is playing in 3D. The ground is the hole's own course map, read from your ROM; the golfer (the game's big swing frames), the pin, the hole's trees and the ball stand on it, the ball at its true height over its ground point with its shadow. The camera follows the game: behind the golfer at the address, after the ball in flight, behind the ball towards the pin on the green, close at the cup. The game's HUD stays on top as the game draws it.
- **Trees**: Courtyard (the default) or Original. Courtyard grows the hole's trees in the design of the courtyard and the restaurant plaza of the Mario Tennis project: egg-shaped crowns of small leaves rising to a point, pale yellow-green with white glints on the lit side, deep green below and round the back, on a trunk with an orange-soil base. Each tree keeps its place and size from the hole and takes the shape that fits: broadleaf eggs, cypress cones, low bushes, palms with drooping fronds at the Palm Club, ribbed cacti with arms at the Dune Club, all in the hole's own colours. They sway in the game's wind and throw shadows in the sun. Original: the old trees (the map's tree pictures stood up and rounded).
- **Grass**: Carpet (the default) or Pixel. Carpet lays a real-looking lawn of grass strands over the course near the camera (as the carpet turf of the Mario Tennis project): mown stripes along the hole on the fairway and tee, a short collar round the green, longer uneven rough in patches with olive and straw strands and tufts, very short fine grass on the green with the game's own slope chevrons kept. Pixel: the look before.
- **Reading the grass**: on the green the grass shows the **slope**: the strands lean and a slow bright band runs **downhill**, more the steeper the spot, exactly where the game's own slope would push the ball (flat spots stay upright and still). The game ignores the wind for a rolling ball, so on the green the wind only stirs the grass a little. Off the green the grass, the flag and the trees show the **wind**: they sway in its direction (the HUD's arrow), bigger and faster the more mph, with gusts running across the long grass.
- **Sand**: Fine (the default) or Pixel. Fine: pale fine sand in the bunkers with soft wind ripples, raked lines along the hole and footprints, rounded outlines with a crisp grass lip and a shaded edge; at the Dune Club the desert round the holes is pale wind-rippled sand with soft dunes under a hazy sky. Pixel: the look before.
- **Ball**: a golf ball at its true size next to the golfer and the cup: dimples, soft light and a highlight from the sun, a printed line that turns as it rolls and spins with the game's spin, a soft contact shadow, a thin outline so it stays visible far off; it drops into a cup in the green.
- **Camera**: Follow (the default), Course (the game's own framing, seen at a slant), Behind, TV.
- **Hills**: soft contours made from the game's slope data (the fairway's slope cells, the green's slope bytes), wooded mounds out of bounds, hollow bunkers. Only drawn: the game plays on its flat values.
- Everything here is render only. With every layer on the game runs frame for frame as with none (web/tests/g3d_layers_same.mjs). Quality **Lite** draws fewer pixels and a coarser ground for phones.
- **Sky** and **Light**: a sky and a horizon for each club (rolling hills at the Marion Club, the sea and islands at the Palm Club, dunes and mesas at the Dune Club, cliffs and a lighthouse at the Links, mountains and a tower at Peach's Castle), at an hour of the round (Hour: Auto by course, the clock, or a fixed hour): the sun's light and its shadows, dusk, night with floodlights on masts at the tee and the green and a lamp at the pin.
- **Terrain**: each terrain type drawn in the hole's own colours with its own surface: mown stripes on the fairway, blades in the rough bending with the game's wind, a mowing check on the green, ripples in the sand, moving water with the sky in it. On the green the putting view's own picture (4x finer) is laid in once the game has shown it.
- **Wear**: divots from irons and wedges off the grass, pitch marks on the green, craters and footprints in the bunkers; they stay for the hole.
- **Ball FX**: a trail by shot type (power shots orange, approach shots blue, putts faint), the spin's seam, a burst of grass or sand at the landing, a splash in the water, a sparkle out of the cup.
- **Ambience**: leaves and grass blown by the game's wind (its speed and direction), mist over water, sand blown off the bunkers, motes in the low sun, fireflies at night.
- **Replay**: a chip-in, a long putt holed, a hole in one or an eagle comes back from a camera following the ball. The game's own shot code is run again on a copy of its memory taken at the launch (the live game is not touched; web/tests/g3d_replay_rerun.mjs checks that every re-run shot follows the game's path frame for frame). A button ends it.
- **Close calls**: a lip-out or a ball that stopped just short of the water gets a review panel in the corner.
- **Big moments**: a hole in one, an albatross or an eagle: fireworks over the green and the camera circling it; a won tournament gets fireworks over the result screen.
- **Gallery**: at tournament rounds people line the ropes round the green and the tee (the game's own map characters, recoloured) and react to the shots; a caddie stands by the golfer in every mode.
- **Title fly-over**: behind PRESS START the 3D view flies over holes of every club, the title's logo and Mario in front.
- **3D overworld** (story mode): the clubhouse rooms, the courses' practice grounds and the Field in 3D while you walk: the blocked cells stand up as walls and counters, the Field's woods and rocks as rounded trees and boulders (hand annotations, `web/js/g3d/field_objects.js`), the people as rounded figures from their own sprites, the menus and message boxes on top. **Fixed**: the people stand upright at their real size on their feet (they lay squashed in the floor, only their heads showing); the message boxes, menus, name plates and portraits show over the 3D rooms (they were missing); the new character's room, the name entry, full-screen menus and fades show the game's own picture instead of an empty 3D plane; no more black floor in the clubhouse; the Field keeps its colours after a fade.
- **Field life**: birds crossing the sky with their shadows, butterflies by day and fireflies at night, dust in the rooms; grass and dirt detail on the ground and a glint on water.
- Quality: the 3D picture's resolution comes down by itself while the pictures come slowly and goes back up when there is room.
