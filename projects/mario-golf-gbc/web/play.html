Load your own Mario Golf (USA).gbc (SHA-1 7f2062e5…). Nothing from the game ships with this page: the code, graphics, courses and music are read from your ROM as it runs. Served from the project folder with the ROM at rom/mario_golf.gbc, it loads by itself.

This page runs the game's own rules rewritten in JavaScript (web/js/golf), with no Game Boy CPU: the shots, the ball, the computer's play, the course drawing, the HUD, the sprites and the sound driver.

It starts at power-on: the opening picture, the demo, the title, then the menus: one player, a preset golfer or a saved character, Stroke or Match, the course. The battery save is kept in this browser in the same place and format as the emulated version's, so a character made there (creating one, the story, Tournament and Training are not ported yet) plays here, and its saves go back. The selector below the screen can also start straight at a round as a new game would: Mario on course 1, a Stroke round of 18 holes, or match play against a computer golfer, with the standings between holes, the scorecard and the result screen. Start while aiming opens the pause menu: all its items work (Camera Mode, Course Map, Scorecard, Settings, Give Up, Quit or Save & Quit). After a round the game goes back to its menus.

Aim with ◀ ▶, change club with ▲ ▼, B for the shot type, Select to look at the pin, A to address the ball, then A for power and A for impact.

Keys: arrows, Z A, X B, Shift Select, Enter Start, P pause the page, Space fast, M mute. Gamepads work too. Emulated version
