Load your own Mario Golf (USA).gbc (SHA-1 7f2062e5…). Nothing from the game ships with this page: the code, graphics, courses and music are read from your ROM as it runs. Served from the project folder with the ROM at rom/mario_golf.gbc, it loads by itself.

This page runs the game's own rules rewritten in JavaScript (web/js/golf), with no Game Boy CPU: the shots, the ball, the computer's play, the course drawing, the HUD, the sprites and the sound driver.

It starts at power-on: the opening picture, the demo, the title, then the menus: one player, a preset golfer or a saved character, Stroke, Match, Tournament (with a saved character: the standings between holes and the placing) or Training (any hole, with Mulligan, Start Over and the wind settings), the course. The battery save is kept in this browser in the same place and format as the emulated version's, so a character made there plays here, and a new character can be made here too (the character select's empty slot: the questions, the name, the first day at the club), and its saves go back (a round left with Save & Quit continues from the saved card). The selector below the screen can also start straight at a round as a new game would: Mario on course 1, a Stroke round of 18 holes, or match play against a computer golfer, with the standings between holes, the scorecard and the result screen. Start while aiming opens the pause menu: all its items work (Camera Mode, Course Map, Scorecard, Settings, Give Up, Quit or Save & Quit). After a round a saved character gets its EXP points and, on a new level, the level-up screen to give its skill points; then the game goes back to its menus. Story mode with a saved character runs as far as the rewrite goes: the clubhouse, walking (B runs) and talking, its doors, the Locker Room and its lockers, the Trophy Room, the Lounge's first visit and its champions (one joins as a partner, follows, plays a Match Game from the Caddie Master's office (a win brings the champions' scene) and can be sent away), the Start menu's text speed, Char. Data, Stars and Save & Quit, the Caddie Master's offices with their club tournaments and practice rounds, the award ceremony after a top-three place (also after a Tournament from the menus), and the Field between the clubs with the Navigation menu and its guide, the Marion Club's driving range, approach range and putting green with their practice, the coaches' lessons and the putting challenge, the Palm, Dune and Links Clubs' driving ranges and approach ranges (with the Links contests), the letter at the tree after the Links is won with the path it opens, the Road to Peach's Castle, the castle's welcome and Peach's Tournament with its award ceremony and, after a win, the ending; the Club Maker's Hut, Windy Valley's targets with the guru, the Tiny-Tots clean-up on the shot clock, the Mini-Mini Lake shot, Raven Woods' crow, the Golf Guru's Pitch and Putt, the Golf Clinic, the hidden friends, the Dune green's golfers with the Alternate Game, and the staff roll after the Links Champion's ceremony. The main menu's Status shows a character's data, stats, VS results and stars and the saved birdies, eagles and holes in one, and Options has the golf dictionary and data management (copy, erase, erase all). The shot records replay a saved hole. Link (the selector) runs two Game Boys side by side joined by a cable in JS: the main menu's VS game on one finds the other (the handshakes, both character selects, the mode and course on the asking side), and the link round plays with each side's shots sent to the other; Tab switches which one the keys play. In the other modes the link game finds no cable partner, as the game does with none. Not ported yet: a few unreferenced and debug-only routines (the project notes list them: REVERSE.md, "Rewrite progress"); the page stops with a message where it reaches a path the rewrite does not have.

Aim with ◀ ▶, change club with ▲ ▼, B for the shot type, Select to look at the pin, A to address the ball, then A for power and A for impact.

Keys: arrows, Z A, X B, Shift Select, Enter Start, P pause the page, Space fast, M mute, Tab the other Game Boy (link). Gamepads work too. Emulated version
